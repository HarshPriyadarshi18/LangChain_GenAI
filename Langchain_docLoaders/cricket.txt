Cricket - Basic Information

Cricket is a popular bat-and-ball sport played between two teams of 11 players each. It originated in England and is now widely played across countries like India, Australia, England, and Pakistan.

Governing Body:
- International Cricket Council (ICC)

Popular Boards:
- Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI)
- England and Wales Cricket Board (ECB)
- Cricket Australia (CA)

Formats of Cricket:
1. Test Cricket
   - Longest format (up to 5 days)
2. One Day International (ODI)
   - 50 overs per side
3. Twenty20 (T20)
   - 20 overs per side

Basic Rules:
- One team bats while the other bowls and fields.
- The batting team tries to score runs.
- The bowling team tries to dismiss batsmen.
- The team with more runs wins.

Ways to Score Runs:
- Running between wickets
- Boundaries (4 runs)
- Sixes (6 runs)

Ways to Get Out:
- Bowled
- Caught
- Run out
- LBW (Leg Before Wicket)
- Stumped

Field Positions:
- Bowler
- Wicketkeeper
- Slip
- Mid-off, Mid-on
- Cover, Point, Fine leg, etc.

Famous Tournaments:
- ICC Cricket World Cup
- ICC T20 World Cup
- Indian Premier League (IPL)

Famous Players:
- Sachin Tendulkar
- Virat Kohli
- MS Dhoni
- Don Bradman
- Ricky Ponting

Equipment Used:
- Bat
- Ball
- Stumps
- Pads, Gloves, Helmet

Interesting Facts:
- Cricket is the most popular sport in India.
- The longest format (Test) is considered the purest form of the game.
- T20 is the fastest and most entertaining format.
